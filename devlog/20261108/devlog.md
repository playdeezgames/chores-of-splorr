Title: Chores of SPLORR!! rebuilt for the browser
Type: Major Update

The cleaning lady is back, in a new browser build.

Chores of SPLORR!! was my GlamJam 2025 entry, made in Defold. It has been rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly. Same house, same dirt, same dust bunnies, same laundry that spoils itself if you look at it wrong. What changed:

- It saves as you play, in this browser, and picks up where you left off.
- The last KEY in the laundry room always appears now. It could fail to, and then you were stuck in the laundry room forever, which is a bit too on the nose.
- When one step says two things, you see both, one after the other.
- The washing machine and dryer no longer flicker, and a full inventory tells you it is full.
- You can tap or click the room to walk, so it works on a phone.

Tomorrow, you can do them again!

This browser version was written with Claude Code (Anthropic's AI coding assistant) at my direction: the port, its tests, the cover (a screenshot of the port) and this post's first draft. The game, its text, its art and its sounds are the January 2025 original's, made without AI.
